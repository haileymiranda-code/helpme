# Wedding Vows — Structure

A 5-part flow: **Serious → Funny → Lovey Dovey → Funny → Serious**.

The emotional swings keep the room locked in. The funny sections make the
serious parts land harder.

---

## 1. SERIOUS — "The truth"

Ground people emotionally. Not life story. Not timeline recap. Just the core
truth.

- You knew he was special immediately
- You loved him before you understood it
- He feels like certainty
- He saw you fully

**Tone:** vulnerable, grounded, sincere, naturally a little poetic.

**Lines that live here:**

- "I think I loved you before I even knew it."
- "You don't make me quieter — you make me bigger."
- "I have never doubted you."

**Goal:** make people emotionally lean in.

---

## 2. FUNNY — "This is who we actually are"

Reset the room. Make the emotional parts hit harder later.

**Tone:** conversational and very specific.

**Material:**

- movie jokes
- overpacking
- debriefing
- buffet engagement
- ChatGPT joke
- your talking vs his acts of service

**Lines that live here:**

- "You show love through acts of service. I show love by turning every car ride into a podcast."
- "You somehow miss the plot of every movie while still being the smartest person I know."
- "We don't just go out — we come home and conduct a full postgame analysis."

**Goal:** everyone thinks, "Oh, this is THEM."

---

## 3. LOVEY DOVEY — "Why him"

The emotional centerpiece. Not generic romance — specific emotional intimacy.

**Tone:** softer, more intimate.

**Themes:**

- partnership
- home
- safety
- everyday love
- choosing each other

**Lines that live here:**

- "My favorite life is the one I have with you in it."
- "Nothing in our story went perfectly to plan, but somehow every road still led me to you."
- "You make ordinary moments feel important to me."

**Goal:** this is where tears happen.

---

## 4. FUNNY — "Bring the room back up"

If you stay emotional too long, people disconnect. Keep charm and personality
alive before the ending lands.

**Tone:** shorter, punchier.

**Material:**

- buffet proposal
- dogs locked in room
- overpacking
- your chaos vs his calm
- talking too much
- random social interactions
- Leo energy

**Line that lives here:**

- "I may be the reason we're late to everything, but you're the reason we survive it."

**Goal:** laughter and affection at the same time.

---

## 5. SERIOUS — "The promise / landing"

Stop performing. Speak directly. Shorter. Cleaner. Slower.

**Do not** add new stories here.

**Land on:**

- certainty
- partnership
- future
- choice

**Lines that live here:**

- "It was never me versus you."
- "I already have everything I want."
- "My plan is simple: to love you every day for the rest of my life."

Then end. One clean emotional hit is stronger than five pretty lines.
